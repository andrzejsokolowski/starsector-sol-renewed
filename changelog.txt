Version 1.3.0 (2026-11-09)
- The crisis now sends one attack per stage, each stronger than the last (50%, 75%, 100% and 150%), instead of up to three at once. Attacks are smaller overall.
- Any of the Sector's factions can now invade Sol, modded ones too, except factions you stand at 30 or better with, your commission faction and the independents.
- Pirates and Pathers come with fewer, better ships. Remnant attacks are smaller.
- Big colonies now raise the crisis meter far more than small ones: nothing up to size 3, 15 and 25 at sizes 5 and 6, 100 from size 10 on.
- Industries count only their own build cost, at 1 point per 200,000 credits (was 50,000, upgrades included).
- Every crisis stage has its own picture, and the crisis texts are rewritten.

Version 1.2.1 (2026-11-08)
- Each Reckoning now makes the crisis meter 1.5 times as long instead of twice (1000, then 1500, 2250, 3375 and so on).
- The meter now stops growing at 8000 points instead of 64000.

Version 1.2.0 (2026-11-08)
- Every Reckoning you survive makes the "Discovering the Past" crisis meter and every mark on it longer, so the next attacks need more attention. Before, the marks never moved and a growing Sol was attacked more and more often.
- New LunaSettings option "Crisis meter growth per Reckoning" (1 to 4, default 2). 1 keeps the old fixed marks.
- The crisis panel shows how long the meter gets after the next Reckoning and how many Reckonings you have survived.

Version 1.1.0 (2026-11-07)
- The Sol music keeps playing while you are docked at a planet or station in Sol or Alpha Centauri, instead of switching to the market music. Fleet encounters and battles keep their own music.
- New LunaSettings toggle "Sol music at planets and stations" (on by default).

Version 1.0.1 (2026-10-25)
- Fixed Sol being left half-built (no Remnants, an empty cryo prison) when it is created alongside a mod that removes the independent faction's portraits.

Version 1.0.0 (2026-10-18)
- First stable release. No gameplay changes since 0.4.0.

Version 0.4.0 (2026-10-18)
- The Discovering the Past crisis meter rises every month instead of once per new colony, size or industry. Each colony size in Sol adds 2 points a month, and each industry adds 1 point a month per 50,000 credits of build cost.
- Fixed the crisis stalling once Sol stopped growing. Existing saves keep their current meter.
