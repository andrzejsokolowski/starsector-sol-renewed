WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
- Adds Sol: the eight planets, Pluto, and the moons that matter, sized and spaced so you can actually fly across it.



Sol is not on the map when you start a game. There is a quest to go and find it, and drag it into the Persean Sector.
Colonize it and the Sector remembers what Sol used to be, and comes to take it off you.
No radiation mechanic. Nothing here is a survey-and-forget rock.



[color=red]Requires [b]LunaLib[/b] and [b]LazyLib[/b].[/color]

[td][hr][center][size=12pt][b]The Quest: "Transferring Sol"[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]From [b]level 10[/b], once your fleet can transverse jump, a researcher in any bar has a lead for you.[/li]
[li]It points at [b]Glasya-Labolas[/b], a rogue Tri-Tachyon AI on Eochu Bres. It has been listening to something transmitting from a pocket of abyssal hyperspace far too dense to jump into, and it is fairly sure the ancient encryption on the signal is Sol.[/li]
[li]It wants something off [b]the Shroud[/b] first. If you have already killed a shrouded ship, it already has what it needs. If not, go into the Abyss and kill one.[/li]
[li]You get a [b]Macro-scale Translocator[/b] and a set of coordinates well past the northern edge of every chart you own. Sit on top of them, switch it on, and Sol arrives in the Sector.[/li]
[li]Not in the mood? Glasya-Labolas will just place Sol for you, and LunaSettings can spawn it with the sector at game start like any other system.[/li]
[/list]
[hr][center][size=12pt][b]The Colony Crisis: "Discovering the Past"[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Every month, your Sol colonies fill a meter in the intel screen. A size 10 colony draws far more eyes than a size 4 one, and industries add a little on top. Nothing lowers it.[/li]
[li]Pirates and the Luddic Path set up bases nearby. Then every stage of the meter brings one attack, each stronger than the last: a raid, a strike force, a war fleet, an armada.[/li]
[li]The attacker can be the pirates, the Luddic Path, the Remnants out of the nests in Sol itself, or one of the Sector's factions, modded ones included. Factions you are on good terms with stay out of it.[/li]
[li]Survive the armada and the meter drops back and gets 1.5 times as long (up to 8000 points), but every attack after it is stronger.[/li]
[li]The attacks never stop on their own. After the first Reckoning, three boss fleets each guard a Domain planetkiller: a pirate warlord, a Luddic Path relic fleet and a Remnant mind out by Neptune. Take all three and no one will strike at Sol again. Their flagships can always be recovered.[/li]
[li]Would rather keep fighting? A button on the mission card brings the attacks back, and stops them again, whenever you like.[/li]
[li]With Moar XIV, Ship and Weapon Pack or Blinding-class Super Battlecarrier installed, the bosses fly an Endless MKII, a Cathedral and a Blinding.[/li]
[li]On, off, meter speed, attack strength and how much longer the meter gets are all in LunaSettings.[/li]
[/list]
[hr][center][size=12pt][b]What Is In The System[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]A hypershunt around the Sun, a gate, a Domain-era mothership, a cryosleeper past Pluto.[/li]
[li][b]Gungnir Dockyard[/b], an abandoned station over Mars with its storage still stocked.[/li]
[li][b]The Inexorable Stasis[/b], a cryo-detention platform holding eight prisoners. Wake one and it flies for you.[/li]

[li][b]Alpha Centauri[/b] (three stars, Proxima b, a guarded nest with three Radiant wrecks) and Beta Centauri a few light-years out.[/li]
[/list]

[spoiler=FAQ]
[b]Where is Sol? I can't find it.[/b]
That is the point. Do the quest, or set "When Sol appears" to "At game start" in LunaSettings.
[b]Can I add this to an existing save?[/b]
Yes. The quest hooks itself up on load and the system is generated when you finish it.
[b]Do I need Ashes of the Domain, Random Assortment of Things or Industrial Evolution?[/b]
No. Nothing is required except LunaLib and LazyLib. If you do have them, Sol picks up the Nidavellir ring on Mars, the Pluto Mining Station, relic conditions, ruins you can restore and seasonal meteor showers. Unknown Skies and Dreams of the Past are supported the same way. Without them you just get fewer conditions.
[b]Is there radiation?[/b]
No.
[b]The Remnants are too much / not enough.[/b]
Four levels in LunaSettings, from none to overrun.
